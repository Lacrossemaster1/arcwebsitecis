Chocolate Delight Cake

by Silvia

Chocolate Delight Cake – Indulge in layers of rich, moist chocolate cake topped with smooth, creamy chocolate frosting. Bursting with decadent chocolate flavor, this irresistible treat is a chocolate lover’s dream and the perfect way to satisfy your sweet tooth!

Ingredients

  • For the base:
  • 20 Oreo Cookies (about 1½ cups crushed)
  • 6 tablespoons butter (melted)
  • For the ganache:
  • 12 oz Semi-Sweet Chocolate (I use 54% cocoa or dark chocolate)
  • 12 oz heavy cream

Instructions

  1. In a small saucepan, heat the cream just until hot (or you can use a microwave in a microwave-safe bowl).
  2. Place the chocolate into a medium mixing bowl.
  3. When the cream is just hot (but not boiling), pour it over the chocolate and whisk until smooth.
  4. Pour the chocolate cream into evenly over the cookie base.
  5. Cover the pan with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least until the dessert is set. Depending on the size of the pan, this may take anywhere from 1 to 3 hours for mini desserts, or 6 to 8 hours for an 8 to 9-inch pan.
  6. Top with whipped cream before serving.
